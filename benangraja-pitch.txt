BENANG RAJA — WHY THIS WEBSITE WAS BUILT

Right now, Benang Raja exists only as scattered marketplace listings
competing on cheap prices. This website rebuilds it as a luxury batik
maison — so customers desire the heritage and craftsmanship before
they ever see a price, letting a new brand command premium value
instead of fighting a price war.

How this site works for the owner:
1. Branding first, selling second — 90% of the site tells the maison
   story, the 21-day canting process, and the premium fabrics; only
   one small "Private Acquisition" section handles transactions.
2. Interactive 3D boutique — customers can "step inside" the boutique
   from their screen, creating an exclusive impression without
   visiting in person.
3. Premium fabric showcase — primisima cotton, silk, and Anjani
   Emerald are presented as heirlooms with weave specifications,
   not like a Shopee catalog.
4. Numbered collection (N°01–N°06) — every piece feels limited,
   signed by its artisan, supporting premium pricing.
